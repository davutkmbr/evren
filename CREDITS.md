## Historic recordings (78 rpm records)

The moments play restored excerpts of historic 78 rpm records. Every original is archived with its source
([`.docs/assets/archive-78rpm.md`](.docs/assets/archive-78rpm.md)).

Public domain (free in the United States and in Turkey), shipped with the game:

- *Kâğıthane Semaisi* and *Felek Bana* — Karekin Proodian, Kemani Minas and ensemble, Victor 69173 and 69175 (New York,
  1916). Library of Congress, National Jukebox.
- *Aya Yorgi apolitikiyonu* (Apolytikion of St George) — Iakovos Nafpliotis, Orfeon (Constantinople, 1913–1918). Transfer:
  analogion.com.

Public domain in Turkey but still protected in the United States; included only in builds made with the private
assets, never in this repository:

- *Kâtibim (Üsküdar'a Gider İken)* — Safiye Ayla (1949). Transfer: Internet Archive.
- *Hüseyni Taksim* — Hafız Kemal Bey, kemençe, Pathé N 11016 (Istanbul, c. 1927–1928). Source gallica.bnf.fr / BnF,
  Archives de la Parole.
- *Hüzzam Taksim* — Reşad Bey, violin, Pathé N 11136 (Istanbul, c. 1927–1928). Source gallica.bnf.fr / BnF, Archives de la
  Parole.
