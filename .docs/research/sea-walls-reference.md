## What the photos show (Commons, category *Propontis Walls*, *Golden Horn Walls, Tower 30*)

- **Masonry** (Propontis wall 1, 2, 17; tower with Greek inscription bands, Commons panoramio 447): small, roughly squared limestone blocks, pale cream to
  buff grey (much lighter than grey granite), 20–35 cm courses, uneven lengths, thick light mortar joints with brick
  chips. Brick bands are thin: usually 3–5 courses, 25–45 cm, orange-red to pink, often wavy and interrupted; the
  spacing between bands varies (1–2 m) and some stretches have none.
- **Repairs everywhere**: whole patches and buttresses in brick, rebuilt stretches in larger ashlar, brick relieving
  arches and filled arches, marble inscription bands (Theophilos) and reused spolia. No two stretches look alike.
- **Lower part** (sea side): the facing is lost over metres at the foot; the rubble-and-mortar core is exposed, with
  large irregular stones and caverns. Boulders (breakwater) lie in front in the water (Propontis wall 1, Samatya).
- **Top**: ragged, almost never a full crenellation; merlons survive only on restored towers (Marble Tower, Yedikule).
  Fig trees, ivy and grass grow from the top and from joints.
- **Towers**: mostly square, projecting 4–6 m, 10–12 m wide, 2–4 m higher than the curtain, small arched windows and
  arrow slits high up, string courses of brick. Pentagonal / prow towers appear on the Marmara and Golden Horn walls.
- **Gates / arches**: round arches with brick voussoir rings over stone jambs; marble door frames at palace gates
  (Bukoleon); many blocked arches.
- **Colour and roughness**: sunlit faces are warm cream; weathering is patchy (dark run-off from the top, black crusts,
  green algae near the ground and sea), rough everywhere, slightly glossy only where damp.
- **Golden Horn** (Ayvansaray, Balat, Fener): fragments between houses, dark and overgrown, houses built against and
  into the wall.

## Kit decisions derived from this

- Default curtain: 12 m high, 4 m thick (sea wall), 1.1 m parapet, merlons mostly missing (kept 30–40 %).
- Towers: square 10–11 m wide, projecting 5 m, 3–4 m above the wall-walk; pentagon / hexagon / octagon variants.
- Stone: CC0 sandstone blocks set (cream) at ~0.28 m courses, tint ~0.55 linear; brick bands 3–5 courses, band spacing
  1.2–2.1 m varying per stretch, missing bands; brick repair patches; lost facing exposing rubble near the foot.
- Foot: battered base of large blocks; sea variant with stepped block plinth and boulder riprap.
- Weathering: run-off streaks, black crust bands, algae near the waterline, plants in joints, grass on ruined tops.
